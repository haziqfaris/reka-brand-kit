# 01 Reconciliation: guidelines v1.0 vs later decisions

`brand-guidelines.html` v1.0 and its `ai-docs/` exports (June 2026) are the REKA master. Since then the owner has made product and wording decisions (7–10 October 2026, recorded in [06](06-decisions-log.md) and in the AntaraX brand kit) that do not yet appear in the guidelines. This page lists every point where the two disagree, so nobody has to guess.

**Until each item is settled, use the column marked "Use for now".** When the owner decides, update `brand-guidelines.html` first (it is the master; the ai-docs are exports of it), re-export the briefs, then delete the row here and log the decision.

## Copy rules that now apply to both brands

These REKA rules have no conflict and should be followed on AntaraX material too (added to the AntaraX kit):

- **"Founder", never CEO**, for Muhammad Haziq Faris, in any context.
- **No em dashes.**
- **Flag illustrative figures** "(illustrative)". REKA material uses no currency figures; AntaraX calculators show money only from the visitor's own inputs (AntaraX kit, 03).
- **"Malaysia's first", never "Southeast Asia's first".**
