# 08 · The website system (25 Oct 2026)

The live reka.re has grown patterns the June guidelines don't describe. Until the guidelines are revised, this page is
the summary and the website is the source. Every item names the file in the `haziqfaris/reka.re` repo
(`prototype/reka-on-clay/…`) that defines it.

## Tokens: the website is the source

`tokens/site.css` and `tokens/site.json` are exported from the website's `assets/base.css` by
`node scripts/export-brand-tokens.mjs` (in reka.re). Do not edit them here; change the website and export again.
`tokens/tokens.css` / `tokens.json` stay as the June transcription of the guidelines, for comparison.

What the website adds over the June tokens:
- **Division colours**, each with ink, background and second tint, used as `data-tone` on a page or card:
  heuristik (blue, AI), harmonik (violet, data and sensing), kinetik (green, mobility), esoterik (mauve, quantum), orange (REKA).
  Colour with meaning only: a division colour marks that division's work, never decoration.
- **Neutrals** the site uses widely: `--muted-2` #7B7974 (labels, meta), `--hairline` #E6E3DC (card edges),
  `--hairline-cool`, `--stone`, `--stone-2`. The page canvas is warm off-white #F7F6F3, ink #1D1D1D.
- Orange #FB9800 is REKA's action and brand colour; the mission line on About is orange (owner's decision, 14 Oct).

## Patterns

| Pattern | What it is | Defined in |
|---|---|---|
| Section grammar | `.blk.chap` sections, `.chap-head` titles, `.tray` grey rounded trays holding white `.card`s, `.related` endings | `assets/base.css`, `assets/pages/company.css` |
| REKA controls | Buttons: label block plus arrow block, chamfered corner; at rest one style, the other on hover | `assets/base.css` |
| Article template | Breadcrumb, headline, meta (author, topic, dates, reading time), In short box, contents, 19px reading column (~70 characters), questions, sources, author box, one dark next-step card, related reading | `assets/pages/article.css`, generated by `scripts/articles.mjs` from `content/articles/*.md` |
| Diagram kit | Line diagrams in SVG with readouts, drawn from `assets/diagrams/*.html` and inlined into pages | `assets/diagram.css`, `docs/strategy/DIAGRAM_KIT.md` |
| The Lab vault door | The Technology page's entrance: armoured doors, 26A, weathering, dents, hex shield; also the reka.tech gate | `assets/realm/vault.css`, `vault.js`; `reka-tech/` |
| Forms | One engine: labels above fields, plain error lines in red under the field, a done card in the form's place | `assets/forms.js` |
| Share cards | 1200 × 630, dark, REKA lockup, orange kicker in JetBrains Mono, Poppins headline | `tools/og.mjs` |
| Motion | Headings rise word by word on section pages (not in articles); reduced motion turns it all off | `assets/motion.js` |

Banned on the site (and they should be banned here too): eyebrows above headlines, empty icon tiles, step numbers as
decoration, blobs, glows, rainbow strips, tilted photos, 3D words, fake UI mocks, filler stats, carousels.

## Logos

The site's single-colour and white versions of the mark, wordmark and lockup (`assets/brand/` on reka.re) are
recoloured stand-ins, not masters. Official mono and white masters are still needed from the designer; when they
arrive they belong in `assets/` here first, then on the site.

## Words

REKA in capitals; "Co-founder", never "Founder"; British spelling; no em dashes.
