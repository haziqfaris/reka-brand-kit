## Services: four ways of working

The guidelines list four services: Consulting / Advisory, Custom Build, Managed AI, Intelligent Automation. reka.re presents them as "Four doors. One way of working.", starting from the visitor's situation:

| Situation | Way | Service | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| "I know AI matters, but not where to start." | 01 **Advise** | Consulting and Advisory | The right move before you spend: assessments, roadmaps, vendor-neutral strategy. |
| "I know what I need. I need it built right." | 02 **Build** | Custom Build and R&D | What doesn't exist yet: software, AI, hardware, robotics, designed for the environment. |
| "I need AI run for me, without a team." | 03 **Managed** | Managed AI | Hosted, monitored and improved on one subscription; runs inside the customer's environment when data can't leave. |
| "I need to automate how my operation runs." | 04 **Automate** | Intelligent Automation | The busywork automated across an operation, with a human in the loop where it matters. |

The constant: "Whichever door, we sell return, not software." Method: see 07 (two versions exist).

## Managed AI

> AI, run for you. You don't need an in-house AI team to get the benefits of AI. We host it, monitor it, improve it and keep it reliable: the models, the infrastructure and the ops, all on one predictable subscription.

Steps: We set it up → You use it → We watch it → We improve it. Promises (only these): one subscription; monitored; improves; private.

**Every AntaraX closed pilot runs as a REKA Managed AI engagement** (Frame, PLExyz Crowd, Angka, Tinara, Loom, Motion).

## REKA and AntaraX

| | REKA | AntaraX |
|---|---|---|
| Is | AI for the physical world: R&D, custom build, Managed AI | AI products for business |
| Verbs | Explore, research, prototype, engineer, validate | Productize, deploy, operate, scale |
| Site | reka.re (also About, careers and legal for AntaraX) | antarax.ai |
| Identity | Orange `#FB9800`, chamfer, HUD | AntaraX blue `#0459F7`, rounded, BaTech |

- **Graduation rule:** a capability moves to AntaraX when it has a repeatable deployment, a published starting price and a fast path to first value. Robotics and autonomy research stay with Kinetik.
- REKA links to AntaraX products as products that came from its research; it does not sell them as REKA products, and does not custom-build a duplicate of one.
- **Public bridge:** "When research becomes repeatable. Some problems we've solved repeatedly deserve to become products. That's why we created AntaraX." Point first to Yara.
- **Legal line on AntaraX material:** "AntaraX, a product line of REKA Inisiatif Sdn Bhd."
