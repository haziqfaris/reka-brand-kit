# 01 Positioning

## Line

> **Built for Progress.**
> We see how technology gets you where you want to go.

## Category

**Technology R&D and innovation partner.** REKA is not primarily a software house, a coding shop, a generic AI consultancy, a training company or a catalogue of technologies.

- **Core philosophy:** technology is not the outcome. Progress is.
- **Commercial expression:** you make more money, move faster, build what others can't.
- **Start with:** "What are you trying to make possible?" Never "Where can we put AI?"
- **AI isn't always the final answer.** A valid result can be "Don't build it."
- If copy could belong unchanged to a generic software agency, rewrite it.

## What makes REKA different

An **independent commercial R&D company**: it funds its own research, owns the IP it creates, commercialises the output as products and services, works across several physical-world domains at once, and stays for the long term. That is uncommon in Southeast Asia, where most serious R&D sits in government institutes, universities or corporate research arms.

The REKA advantage: see what others don't · connect what others don't · prove before committing · build what doesn't exist · own the outcome.

**Values:** Think radically. Build with empathy. Bring real know-how. Own the outcome.

**Agnostic by design:** technology agnostic and industry agnostic as a brand; campaigns may focus on verticals (professional services, manufacturing, property, logistics, mobility, infrastructure, selected oil and gas) without making them REKA's identity.

**Global-first:** Malaysia is origin and proof, not a limit. Avoid "Malaysia's leading…" as the proposition.

## REKA and AntaraX

| | REKA | AntaraX |
|---|---|---|
| Is | R&D, innovation, strategic implementation, Managed AI | AI products for business |
| Verbs | Explore, research, prototype, engineer, validate | Productize, deploy, operate, scale |
| Site | reka.re (also hosts About, story, careers and legal for both) | antarax.ai |

- **Graduation rule:** a capability moves to AntaraX when it has a repeatable deployment, a published starting price and a fast path to first value. Until then it stays at REKA. Robotics and autonomy stay with REKA's **Kinetik** division and are not AntaraX products.
- REKA links to AntaraX products as "products that came from REKA's research"; it does **not** sell them as REKA products.
- If an AntaraX product already solves most of a customer's problem, REKA does not custom-build a duplicate.
- **Managed AI is REKA's service**, and every AntaraX closed pilot runs as a Managed AI engagement.
- **Public bridge:** "When research becomes repeatable. Some problems we've solved repeatedly deserve to become products. That's why we created AntaraX." Point first to Yara, AntaraX's lead product.
- **Legal line on AntaraX material:** "AntaraX, a product line of REKA Inisiatif Sdn Bhd."
