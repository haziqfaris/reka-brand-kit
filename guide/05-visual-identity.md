# 05 Visual identity

Values in [`tokens/`](../tokens/). Reference implementation: `haziqfaris/reka.re`, `reka-site-full.html`.

## Character

**Professional, minimal cyberpunk.** Dark, precise and engineered, with a little REKA orange glowing through. It should feel curious, exploratory, experienced, multidisciplinary, ambitious and human: a lab at night, not a neon arcade.

## Logo

- The **REKA mark**: a circle cut by a long diagonal slash that runs past the circle at both ends, with parallel strokes, the lowest of which steps up like a rising line. REKA orange `#FB9800` on its own; white on dark in the lockup. In BaTech illustrations (AntaraX) the mark is traced with its long slash sharp at both ends, as the maker's signature.
- The **wordmark** "REKA" in widely tracked capitals, the A drawn as an open chevron with no crossbar (`assets/logo/reka-wordmark-white.svg`, the mark and wordmark together, coloured with `currentColor`): white on dark by default; orange or near-black where needed.
- Clear space: at least the radius of the mark's circle on every side.
- **Favicon tile:** the orange mark on `#17142B` with 14px corners (`assets/logo/reka-favicon.svg`).
- Legal name in small print: REKA Inisiatif Sdn Bhd.
- Don't recolour the mark outside orange, white or near-black; don't stretch, rotate, outline or add effects.

## Colour

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Ground | `#0B0B0C` (raised `#161618`) |
| Ink | `#F1F1F0`, secondary `#B9B9BD`, tertiary `#86868C` |
| **REKA orange** | `#FB9800`, deep `#D97F00` |
| Accents (sparingly, one per section) | cyan `#46D6C8`, teal `#2BB3A3`, violet `#6D5AE0`, blue `#2B6CF0` |

**The orange gradient** (`150deg`, light orange to `#D97F00`) marks the few things that matter on a page: a hero word, the key CTA, a product highlight. On REKA-hosted product pages it may tint the hero. A little goes a long way: never full-page, never behind body text.

Hairlines are orange fading to transparent at both ends, or white at 10% opacity.

## Type

- **Poppins** for headlines and body; headlines often pair a plain clause with an emphasised one ("Four doors. *One way of working.*").
- **JetBrains Mono** used heavily: labels, data, step numbers ("Way 01 · Stages 01–02"), `//` section markers, captions.

## Shape and texture

- **Sharp:** corners 2–3px (9px maximum). Contrast with AntaraX's rounded 20–28px cards.
- Faint engineering grids, scan lines, technical callouts, coordinates and drawing marks at very low contrast.
- The REKA story page uses **engineering drawings and blueprint lines** with real photos of the car and the lab.

## Imagery

Prefer real machines, prototypes, labs, people building, autonomous systems, robotics, sensing, diagrams and technical artefacts. Avoid generic SaaS visuals, stock "digital transformation", glowing brains and giant technology-logo walls.
