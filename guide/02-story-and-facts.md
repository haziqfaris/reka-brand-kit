## The through-line

From design studio, to teaching every tool, to a self-driving car that forced deep capability in AI, sensing, mapping, real-time systems and machines, to applying that across industries, to products. Two lessons carried forward:

- **Most operational work still needs people;** the value is seeing every task live and letting a few people steer many (this became Yara).
- **Sensing the physical world** (from the car's sensors and edge computing) became smart-city sensing, crowd analytics and then PLExyz.

## Claims ownership

- "Malaysia's first self-driving car on open roads" is **REKA's** claim; never "Southeast Asia's first". AntaraX does not use it; AntaraX says "the work REKA began with its 2016 self-driving car".
- REKA's story page gets its **own style** from the guidelines' graphic language: HUD primitives (readout bars, hatches, brackets, panels, capsules, ticks) over the dot grid, the mark's explode/assemble as a storytelling device, and real photos of the car and the lab. (AntaraX's story uses BaTech.)
- "Reka" means *to design, to invent* in Malay.
