# REKA brand kit

**REKA Inisiatif Sdn Bhd** · AI for the physical world. · Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia · reka.re · holla@reka.re

The single reference for how REKA looks, sounds and is organised. Every REKA site, document, deck, product UI and campaign follows this kit. AntaraX, REKA's AI product line, has its own kit: [haziqfaris/AntaraX-brand-kit](https://github.com/haziqfaris/AntaraX-brand-kit).

## The master

**[`brand-guidelines.html`](brand-guidelines.html)** (v1.0) is the single visual master. Open it in a browser: foundation, logo, colour, typography, graphic language, applications, digital UI and resources, with copy-to-clipboard swatches and download buttons.

Everything else here derives from it. **Change the guidelines first, always**, then re-export the briefs and update the tokens.

## The one-minute version

- **Line:** AI for the physical world. **Supporting:** Born on Southeast Asian roads. Built for global operations. **Mission:** Enable people to create.
- **Divisions:** Harmonik (sensing), Kinetik (autonomy), Heuristik (AI applications, home of AntaraX), Esoterik (digital transformation).
- **Services:** Advise, Build, Managed AI, Intelligent Automation.
- **Look:** orange `#FB9800` as a 10% accent; near-black `#0B0B0C` for internal and brand surfaces, warm light `#F7F6F3` for public ones; Poppins and JetBrains Mono; chamfer cuts, never border-radius; HUD primitives over a dot grid; the official and public stripes.
- **The mark:** one disc holding three forces: Meniscus (Order), Scalpel (Discovery), Lightning (Chaos).
- **Hard copy rules:** no em dashes; no currency figures; flag illustrative numbers; "Malaysia's first", never "Southeast Asia's first"; "Founder", never CEO; no fabricated metrics or partnerships.

## Known disagreements

The guidelines (June 2026) predate the October portfolio decisions: PLExyz and Tinara now sit in AntaraX, AntaraX is a product line with six families, and some proof wording differs. **[guide/01 Reconciliation](guide/01-reconciliation.md)** lists every point and what to use until the guidelines are updated.
