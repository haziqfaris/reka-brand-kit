# REKA Product Families
> Division structure, product descriptions, and family colour mapping.
> Updated: June 2026

## Structure
REKA has four divisions. Each owns one colour family and one flagship product.

---

## Harmonik — Sensing & Digitalization
**Colour family:** Purple (`#6040CC` primary TBC)
**Flagship product:** PLExyz

### PLExyz
Crowd analytics and spatial intelligence platform. Real-time occupancy sensing, zone density mapping, and footfall analysis for physical spaces (transit, retail, venues, smart cities).

**Key features:** Live zone density heatmap, occupancy tracking, dwell-time analytics, sensor grid management, automated reporting.

**Colour ramp:**
- Light: `#C8BDE8` · Mid: `#865ACE` · Primary: `#6040CC` (TBC) · Dark/nav: `#281B4F`

---

## Kinetik — Autonomy + Physical Automation
**Colour family:** Teal (`#1AB89C` primary TBC)
**Flagship product:** OVERride

### OVERride
Autonomous vehicle and fleet management platform. Real-time fleet control, route management, and telemetry for autonomous and semi-autonomous physical systems.

**Key features:** Live unit status grid, telemetry monitoring, route adherence tracking, maintenance scheduling, alert management.

**Colour ramp:**
- Light: `#BAE4E1` · Mid: `#6AD1BF` · Primary: `#1AB89C` (TBC) · Dark/nav: `#063028`

---

## Heuristik — AI Driven Application
**Colour family:** Blue (`#1268F7` primary)
**Flagship product:** AntaraX (formerly MiC / Mission Control)

### AntaraX
AI operations and inference management platform. Manages the full AI pipeline from data ingest to on-device deployment.

**Sub-products:**
- **Loom** — Data ingest pipeline
- **Ripple** — Streaming inference
- **Frame** — Vision/computer vision models
- **Edge** — On-device / edge deployment

**Colour ramp:**
- Light: `#6AAEFF` · Mid: `#0496FF` · Primary: `#1268F7` · Dark/nav: `#0D2850`

---

## Esoterik — Digital Transformation & Humanizing Technology
**Colour family:** Maroon (`#501643` primary)
**Flagship product:** Tinara

### Tinara
Business intelligence and performance analytics. Currency-free indexed performance tracking, segment analysis, and reporting.

**Key features:** Performance index, conversion tracking, multi-source data sync, segment comparison, scheduled reports.

**Colour ramp:**
- Light: `#CAB9C7` · Mid: `#8D6885` · Primary: `#501643` · Dark/nav: `#2D0A22`

---

## REKA Generic UIs

### Internal console (console.reka.re)
Cross-division operations dashboard. Dark mode (`#131318` content). Orange (`#FB9800`) accent. Shows all four divisions' live deployments and project counts.

### Client portal (app.reka.re)
External client-facing portal. Light mode (`#F7F6F3`). Orange accent. Shows active products, usage, billing, support.
