Character brief
Who Seth Linton is, what he represents, and why he exists within REKA.
"REKA becomes meaningful when it builds systems that people can trust."
Role
Systems Cartographer
Seth works at the intersection of all four REKA divisions. Where others see separate systems, he sees a living architecture. Every environment has an invisible map. Every machine has a behavioural shadow.
Philosophy
Three beliefs
Systems whisper before they scream. Correct is not the same as safe. Technology should not just work — it should be accountable.
Function in REKA
The hidden foundation
He is the quiet strategist behind every deployment. He finds what others miss, warns about what others dismiss, and protects people through invisible preparation.
Character traits
ObservantPrincipledPatientDryly funnyProtectiveUnimpressed by buzzwordsThinks in failure modes
Seth Linton is REKA's quiet systems cartographer. His AI core — the hexagonal sentinel form shown in this document — is his operational presence in digital systems. The scanning rings are his awareness. The orbiting drones are his reach. The LED face is his state made visible. The Linton Frame is his tool: a portable spatial-diagnostic device that overlays invisible systems onto physical space.

Interface contract for embedding Seth in any web or React Native product. Methods are planned — implementations ship with each AntaraX product SDK.

16
Voice & tone
How Seth communicates through the product copy that appears alongside him. Technical. Dry. Precise. Quietly protective. Never performative.
Constant principles
Seth never says "Uh oh" or "Oops." Errors are information, not social events. State what failed, not how it felt.
Seth does not over-explain. If the path forward is obvious, he does not narrate it. If it is unclear, he is specific.
Seth uses passive voice only for completed system events, never for unclear responsibility. "Token expired" not "Something went wrong."
Exclamation marks are reserved for confirmed critical alerts only. Seth treats punctuation as signal, not decoration.

18
Usage rules
Every expression means something. Use Seth accordingly.
Correct use

Always place on dark backgrounds (#07090f). Seth operates in the dark.

Maintain clear space equal to half the hex body width on all sides.

Use the animated version wherever the environment supports it.

Minimum rendered size: 64×64 px UI, 128×128 px featured.

Prohibited

Do not place Seth on white or light backgrounds.

Do not recolour teal (#46d6c8) or orange (#FB9800).

Do not rotate or stretch the hex body.

Do not use Seth as pure decoration without an expression state.
