## 0. Founder-confirmed early history (2026-10-09)

Given directly by the founder, Muhammad Haziq Faris Hasnol. Treat as REKA-only, but authoritative for REKA's own pages.

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2013 | Founded as a graphics and web design company (REKA Studios) |
| 2014 | Focus moved to technology |
| 2014–2015 | Ran a lot of training: web development, electronics (Raspberry Pi, Arduino), IoT, and mobile apps (PhoneGap, AppDroid) |
| 2016 | Moved into early AI: convolutional neural networks (CNN), optimisation, image processing, digital signal processing (DSP). Self-driving car work started in September 2016 |

| 2018–2021 | Main effort moved from autonomous cars to software automation, because of commercialisation roadblocks, regulation, regional infrastructure, market adoption and culture. AV work continued: government ties across Southeast Asia and frameworks for deploying autonomous vehicles quickly in different situations, to raise awareness and help regional AV startups |
| Pandemic | Serious turn to automation, RPA and robotics so organisations could keep operating remotely; helped companies such as UMW Industries and Murata Electronics run tasks remotely |

Owner decisions (2026-10-09):
- "Malaysia's first self-driving car": use on REKA only, not on AntaraX.
- Frost & Sullivan: REKA won, but did not pay for the certificate. Say "recognised by Frost & Sullivan", not "award".

This settles the 2013 vs 2014 founding conflict in section 4. The full narrative draft is in `docs/content/REKA_STORY.md`.

## 2. The 2016 self-driving car (CRETA)

**Name.** CRETA = "Conscious Reka Transport Apparatus" (futuristiccar.wordpress.com; REKA press release 2017). It was described as a retrofit apparatus for human-driven cars. The CRETA blog claims level 4 as the target. In the same period Haziq described the system as "Category 3" (paultan) and said the planned kit would be "Level 2 and 3" by 2020 (CRETA blog).

**Timeline of the build.**
- Development began September 2016 (paultan; MIDA).
- The first prototype, a **Proton Waja**, started testing in **November 2016** (paultan; Says).
- The KL–Melaka driverless run took place "around a month after that" (paultan), so about December 2016. REKA's own wording is simply "2016".
- The CRETA blog says the system was built in "about two months".
- CRETA was first shown publicly at **KLESF, November 2016** (REKA press release).

**Route and direction.**
- paultan.org (Dec 2017): "from KL to Melaka with two passengers in the rear seats and no driver."
- CIO.com: "from Kuala Lumpur to Melaka with two passengers in the back seats and no driver."
- REKA press release (2017) and reka.re: "from Melaka to Kuala Lumpur", on the **PLUS Highway** per reka.re.
- The PLUS Highway is named **only by reka.re**. No independent source found names the highway.
- **Distance: not found** in any REKA-specific source. (A generic travel page gives KL–Melaka as about 147 km. That figure is not evidence of the test.)
- Pandulaju (Malay) says only that test drives were done "around Kuala Lumpur and Melaka".

**Vehicle.**
- Proton Waja: the first prototype (paultan; Says).
- Proton Perdana: per MIDA's 2016 reference. Two Perdana units loaned by MARA and NanoMalaysia were used in 2017 for public demos (paultan; Says).
- Proton Wira and Proton Perdana: per a 2019 academic paper.
- paultan says there were three prototype cars in total.

**Hardware and sensors.**
- **RIG (Reka Innovation Gear):** a self-developed IoT development board / "electronic chip" acting as the controller (Says; paultan; REKA press release). One snippet says RIG development started January 2016.
- Sensors: "existing off-the-shelf technology such as cameras (they started off with a dashcam), radar sensors and laser" (paultan quoting Haziq). Also GPS and a video camera (Says). The CRETA blog mentions a Logitech web camera.
- Software: "self-developed smart software" (Haziq, paultan).

**Performance claims (paultan via search summary).** Optimal operating speed of 60–80 km/h, turning at 20–30 km/h, braking within 5–10 m. Navigation relied on standard tools such as Google Maps.

**Maturity at the time.** "20% complete/ready" (REKA press release 2017; CRETA blog). About 30% ready (deenamik.com blog 2017). The system "can drive itself but is still far from being considered safe" (Pandulaju).

**People.**
- **Muhammad Haziq Faris Hasnol**, founder and CEO, aged 27 in Dec 2017 (paultan; Says; REKA press release).
  - paultan BM and Says state he studied Computer Science at Cambridge without finishing, and worked at Google in the US.
  - A search summary attributes "over a year" on Google Chrome to Says. That detail is unverified.
- **Mohd Amri Mohd Din**, COO (2023 coverage; LinkedIn lists him as "Managing Partner & COO").
- No other 2016 team members were found by name.

**Media coverage found.**
- paultan.org: EN 6 Dec 2017 and BM 5 Dec 2017
- Says.com (2017)
- motiondigest.com: 27 Nov 2017 and 22 Jan 2018
- CIO.com (Southeast Asia AV overview; date not retrieved)
- Pandulaju.com.my
- deenamik.com (blog, Oct 2017)
- MIDA article
- ResearchGate 2019 paper
- vulcanpost (mentions CRETA in a UTM–MooVita story)

**No 2016 news coverage of the drive itself was found.** The earliest independent reports are from late 2017, and they relay REKA's account.

**"Malaysia's first self-driving car on a public road".**
- The claim appears in REKA's own press release and on reka.re.
- The MIDA-hosted deck ("Launched Malaysia's First Self-Driving Car") was authored by REKA.
- No independent source confirms the "first" claim.
- For context, Malaysia's first government-approved AV public test route (Cyberjaya MyAV, 7 km) came only in late 2020 (MIDA; OpenGov Asia; Digital News Asia). The 2016 drive therefore pre-dates any formal AV trial framework.

## 3. Partners, products, awards, press

**PLExyz.** REKA's private-by-design crowd and audience analytics sensor, edge-computed and counting without identifying individuals (reka.re; Crunchbase).
- Deployed with MedAds Media on clinic screens in KL (reka.re).
- reka.re says EXP-04 "graduated" into PLExyz. No independent source mentions EXP-04.

**Frost & Sullivan 2022 "Best Crowd Analytics Solution".**
- Found **only on reka.re**.
- Frost & Sullivan's public material shows the Asia-Pacific Crowd Analytics for Smart City Solutions award going to **Fusionex Group** (2021 award; write-up PDF dated Jan 2022).
- REKA was not found on Frost & Sullivan's 2022 recipients page snippet, nor in any Malaysia Excellence Awards listing.
- **Status: unverified.** Ask REKA for the certificate or award letter before publishing.

**Murata.**
- Independent press confirms a REKA–Murata partnership on LiDAR traffic counters: Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia in phase 1, Vietnam and the Philippines in phase 2; sensors at Jalan Kuching and LDP; claimed to be "25% more effective" than CCTV and privacy-preserving. Sources: newshubasia; disruptivetechasia; caricarz.
- Murata's own March 2021 release mentions expansion to Malaysia but, in the snippet, does not name REKA.
- The 2019 start date is REKA-only. A search summary says the pilot ran 2019–2021.

**Autoware Foundation.**
- Membership is confirmed by autoware.org's post "REKA joins the Autoware Foundation!", dated 1 July 2024. The post calls REKA "an experienced Autoware integrator", mentions AMRs in Singapore (indoor and outdoor), and says REKA planned to join the Open AD Kit, API, and localization/mapping working groups.
- "Contributing since 2021" is REKA-only.
- One search summary said reka.re lists the membership under a 2017 milestone. That is probably a layout artefact, but check the live site.

**AMRs in Singapore.** Confirmed by the Autoware announcement (2024).

**ELMLAB.** MoU dated 18 March 2022 (Business Today). ELMLAB builds the connectivity foundation and REKA builds the self-driving features, enabling teleoperation, starting with REKA's own vehicles. elmlab.my headlines it "autonomous vehicle security", but the body text is about connectivity. No outcome reported.

**SERV.** REKA became part of SERV (SERV Technology Sdn Bhd / "SERV Group"; PitchBook acquisition date 1 Dec 2019; SERV CEO Arief Imran). One article calls REKA "a wholly owned subsidiary of SERV X".

**NanoMalaysia and MARA.** Two Proton Perdana units were loaned for prototypes (paultan; Says; independent). The NanoMalaysia co-working and NICE Expo 2017 demo are REKA-only.

**MARii.**
- Public rides at Malaysia Autoshow 2017 were run in collaboration with MARii (Says).
- MARii's 2023 Autoshow wrap-up mentions Malaysian companies including REKA showcasing autonomous driving (MARii site).

**Other.**
- MAGnicas security product co-developed with MAGnet Security (REKA-only).
- reka.re mentions a Cyberjaya public-route industry validation (REKA-only).
